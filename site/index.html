What it handles

  • $…$, $$…$$, \(…\), \[…\] in body, tables, headers, footers, footnotes.
  • Keeps run formatting around the math. Dollar amounts such as $0.35 to $0.60 are left alone.
  • Equations it cannot parse stay as the original text and are counted in the result.
